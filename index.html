Briefing

How to play

  1. 01

    Camera check

    Kneel next to a cushion, webcam at your side. We check it can see your arms.

  2. 02

    The right calls

    Is it safe? Does he respond? Is he breathing? Who do you call? Answer before the clock runs out.

  3. 03

    Chest compressions

    Push on the cushion. The webcam reads your rhythm, depth and arms, and coaches you live.

  4. 04

    Your grade

    The ambulance takes over. You get a score and one tip for next time.

You need: a firm cushion, a webcam that sees you from the side, space to kneel, sound on, and two minutes.
